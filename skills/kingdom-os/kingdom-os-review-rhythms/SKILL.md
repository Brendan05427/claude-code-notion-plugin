# Kingdom OS Review Rhythms

Strategic review is how a Kingdom leader governs rather than reacts. Each rhythm operates at a different altitude and time horizon.

## The Four Review Cadences

| Cadence | Frequency | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Strategic Review | Every Sunday (or chosen day) | Govern the important, not just the urgent | 45–60 min |
| Monthly Kingdom Audit | First of each month | Deep correction across all 10 domains | 90 min |
| 90-Day Sprint Review | Every 13 weeks | Assess quarterly goals, reset targets | 2–3 hours |
| Annual Kingdom Review | Year end | Measure movement, architect next year | Half day |

---

## Weekly Strategic Review

**Purpose**: Stop managing the urgent and begin governing the important.

### Steps

1. **Domain Health Scores (1–5)**
   Score each of the 7 core domains:
   - Spiritual, Family, Business, Health, Finance, Ministry, Leadership

2. **Average Health Score** = average of all 7 scores

3. **RAG Status**
   - 4–5: Green
   - 3: Watch
   - 2: Amber
   - 1: Red

4. **Top Win**
   - What was the highest-leverage win from the past week?

5. **Primary Blocker**
   - What is the single biggest obstacle to Kingdom progress this week?

6. **3–5 Featured Actions**
   - Choose this week's highest-leverage actions (not tasks — assignments)

7. **Delegation / Support Needed**
   - What do you need from others this week?

8. **Mark Review Complete**: Yes

Log to: Weekly Strategic Review database in Notion for the current week start date.

## Monthly Kingdom Audit

**Purpose**: Deep correction across all life domains. The goal is not shame; it is ordered correction.

### Steps

Score all 10 domains (1–5):
1. Spiritual
2. Family
3. Business
4. Health
5. Finance
6. Ministry
7. Leadership
8. Learning
9. Stewardship
10. Rest

Then capture:
- **Average Score** across all 10
- **RAG Status** based on average
- **Correction Focus** — the single domain most needing reorder this month
- **Next Obedience Step** — one concrete action to install order in the correction domain

Log to: Monthly Kingdom Audit database for the current month.

## 90-Day Sprint Planner

**Purpose**: Plan the next quarter around Kingdom assignment, not noise.

### Sprint Identity Fields
- **Quarter**: Q1–Q4
- **Sprint Theme**: One phrase that names this season (e.g., "Order before multiplication")
- **Kingdom Assignment**: What God is specifically tasking this quarter
- **Start Date / End Date**
- **Primary Scripture**: The anchor passage for the quarter

### 90-Day Goals
For each goal, capture:
- Domain
- Kingdom Outcome (specific result)
- Why It Matters
- Owner
- Priority (P0–P2)
- Start and Due dates
- Progress %
- Status
- Next Milestone
- Next Action

### Weekly Targets
For each of the 13 weeks:
- Primary Target
- Milestone
- Success Measure
- Planned Deep Work Blocks
- Actual Progress
- RAG

Log to: 90-Day Sprint Planner database in Notion.

## Annual Kingdom Review

**Purpose**: Measure movement across all domains and architect the next year from evidence, not aspiration.

### Domain Review (per domain)
- Starting Score (beginning of year)
- Ending Score (end of year)
- Movement (ending − starting)
- Evidence of Growth
- Regret / Lesson
- Breakthrough
- Next-Year Focus
- RAG

### Year-End Reflection Prompts
- **Identity**: Who did I become this year, and where did I resist formation?
- **Family**: Did my family get a more present version of me this year?
- **Business**: Where did I build by instruction rather than instinct?
- **Stewardship**: How did I handle money, influence, people, and time as tests?
- **Second Brain**: What did I capture, organize, distill, and express that created real fruit?
- **Next Year**: What order must be installed before the next level of multiplication?

### Annual Summary
- Average Starting Score
- Average Ending Score
- Average Movement
- Strongest Domain
- Most Improved Domain

Log to: Annual Kingdom Review database in Notion.

## Review Execution Principles

- Reviews are governance sessions, not performance reviews
- A low score is a correction signal, not a condemnation
- Every review must produce at least one concrete next obedience step
- Domain scores drift without honest review — score with integrity
- The Weekly Review feeds the Monthly Audit; the Monthly feeds the Quarterly Sprint
- Nothing in the Second Brain is complete until it has been distilled into a governed action
